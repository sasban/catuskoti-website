What we do

Two ways to work with us.

01 · Land & mining partnerships

Gem-bearing land in the Ratnapura district

Experienced, licensed mining teams can work our land on a revenue-share basis. Every parcel is sold at open auction, so the price is clear to everyone, and settlements are written up and shared after each sale.

  • Revenue share agreed upfront, no hidden costs
  • Open auctions with competing bidders
  • Written settlement after every auction
Enquire about land

02 · Rough & trade

Rough lots and cut parcels for dealers

Natural Ceylon sapphire, spinel, chrysoberyl and more, straight from our own mines. We sell rough lots at auction and cut stones in parcels to the trade, in Colombo and abroad.

  • Rough: sapphire, geuda, pushparaga, mixed lots
  • Cut parcels: sapphire and spinel, mostly unheated
  • Viewing in Colombo by appointment

About

Science and the soil.

Catuṣkoṭi is led by Sasanka Bandara, a chemist from the second generation of our family’s gem-mining business. Before returning to mining he spent many years in colour and manufacturing, including as General Manager at MAS Brands for Sri Lanka and Bangladesh.

He holds a BSc (Hons) in Chemistry from the University of Colombo, an MBA from the University of Sheffield and the Advanced Management Program from Columbia Business School.
