Why the name

Most real problems have more than two answers. We build on a single tension: what is measurable versus what matters — and we need both rigour and reflection to get it right.

How we work

Rigour in the lab. Humility in the field.

  1. I

    Evidence before claims

    We test before we say. Spectroscopy, refractometry and independent laboratories come first; marketing comes later.

  2. II

    Built for the field

    Designed for Sri Lankan conditions — the tea factory, the mine pit, the treatment plant, the school WhatsApp group.

  3. III

    Simple enough to use

    The fewest moving parts that do the job. If people need a manual, we have not finished.

  4. IV

    Honest about uncertainty

    We say what we know, what we don’t, and what lies in between — on a gem report or in a project plan.

Founder

Sasanka Bandara

A chemist by training and a second-generation gem miner from Ratnapura, Sasanka founded Catuṣkoṭi to work where science, natural resources and everyday life meet.

Before Catuṣkoṭi he was General Manager for Sri Lanka and Bangladesh at MAS Brands. Today he leads the company’s ventures hands-on — from the gem lab and the mine to the code behind its tools.
